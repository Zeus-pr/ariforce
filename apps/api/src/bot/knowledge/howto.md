# How to find things

Answer these from this text. Do not invent a figure. If they also want the number, use the matching tool, and only for people on their line. A page shows the same scope as the rest of AriForce: a manager sees their team, and a Director or Distribution Head sees the company. System Admin can ask, and sees the same line the rest of AriForce gives them, which is not the company.

- How do I find this month’s sales figures? Open Sales in the sidebar. Primary is sales into the stockist. Secondary is sales out of the stockist. You can also ask ARIBOT “What is primary sales this month?” or “What is secondary sales this month?” A headquarters, such as Suri, is a place: ask “What are October sales for Suri?”
- How do I find primary sales? Open Sales and read the primary column for the month. Or ask “What is primary sales this month?”
- How do I find secondary sales? Open Sales for the summary, or AWS & SS for a stockist’s statement. Or ask “What is secondary sales this month?”
- How do I find this month’s coverage? Open Coverage. Coverage is the share of MSL doctors visited at least once. Or ask “What is my team’s coverage this month?”
- How do I find missed doctors? Open Coverage and read the missed-doctor share. That is MSL doctors with no visit. Or ask “What share of doctors were missed this month?”
- How do I find frequency compliance? Open Coverage. It is the share of MSL doctors visited at least as often as required. Or ask “What is frequency compliance this month?”
- How do I find the call average? Open Team Performance or Reports. Call average is doctor calls on field days divided by field days. Or ask “What is the call average this month?”
- How do I find DCR compliance? Open Team Performance or Reports. It is filed DCR days against expected working days. Or ask “What is DCR compliance this month?”
- How do I find whether DCRs were on time? A DCR is on time when it is submitted by the end of the next day, India time. Ask “What share of DCRs were on time this month?”
- How do I find tour plan adherence? Open Tour Plans for the plan, or ask “What is tour plan adherence this month?” Adherence is a planned visit that has a call on the same outlet and the same date.
- How do I find POB? POB is the order value booked on a call. Ask “What is POB this month?” Product-wise POB is under the product question.
- How do I find which products were detailed? Ask “Which products were detailed this month?” The answer names the products that were detailed, sampled, or booked, and the ones that led.
- How do I find my team? Open My Team in the sidebar, or ask “Who is on my team?”
- How do I find who has no manager? Ask “Who on my line has no manager?” Unassigned people appear only when they sit in your territory and rank below you.
- How do I find a doctor? Open Doctors. You see doctors on your territories, including your team’s territories.
- How do I find a chemist? Open Chemists. The same territory rule applies.
- How do I find a stockist? Open Stockists. Distribution figures for a stockist’s statement are under AWS & SS.
- How do I file a DCR? Open DCR. A Business Manager files the day’s work. A manager reviews the team’s submitted reports.
- How do I make a tour plan? Open Tour Plans, fill the month, and submit. It goes to your manager.
- How do I apply for leave? Open Leave, apply, and submit. It goes up your manager chain. You never decide your own leave.
- How do I submit an expense? Open Expenses, keep it as a draft until it is ready, then submit. Your manager decides it. You never decide your own claim.
- How do I approve something waiting? Open Approvals. You can decide requests from people below you, never your own.
- How do I find exceptions? Open Exceptions. It lists the month’s attention items, such as low calls or a missing DCR, for the people you can see.
- How do I find the SFC? Open SFC. It is the standard fare chart, the travel routes used for allowance. You see your own routes, and a manager sees the line’s routes.
- How do I see the company total? Only a Director or Distribution Head can. Open Dashboard or Sales, or ask “What is company coverage this month?” Anyone else who asks for the company is refused.
